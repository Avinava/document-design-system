Incident review · INC-2026-07-30

One full queue stopped every ingestion producer

For 1 hour 52 minutes, a single saturated queue turned local backpressure into a platform-wide halt. Consumer scaling restored service; it did not remove the failure mode.

02

Cause, not symptom

3 links

Trigger

Burst exceeded drain rate

Checkout traffic rose while one consumer was recycling.

Amplifier

One queue held every producer

Healthy producers had no independent path or bounded shed behavior.

Outcome

Backpressure reached the gateway

Requests blocked until clients saw timeouts and 503 responses.

Root cause: ingestion had one queue and no failure boundary. Scaling consumers changed recovery time, not the architecture that allowed one queue to stop all producers.

03

Response review

people + process

Worked

The runbook found the right control

The on-call opened the lag dashboard immediately and the documented scale command was safe to repeat.

Failed

The alert described lag, not blast radius

The first page did not say every producer shared the queue, delaying escalation until hard failures began.

Detection also waited for five minutes above 120 seconds. That protected against noisy pages but consumed most of the buffer before a human could respond.
