# Accessibility contract

These rules apply to every skill in this repo. They are not a final checklist item — most of them are cheaper to satisfy while building than to retrofit.

The underlying principle: **no piece of meaning may exist only in color, only in position, or only in a hover state.** A document that violates this is unreadable to some readers, illegible when printed in grayscale, and meaningless when a chart is screenshotted into a chat.

## Inline SVG

Every chart and diagram needs a title and description, wired to the `<svg>`:

```html
<svg role="img" aria-labelledby="chart-fn-title chart-fn-desc" viewBox="0 0 720 320">
  <title id="chart-fn-title">Current footprint by function</title>
  <desc id="chart-fn-desc">Nine functions ranked by size. Ingestion holds 41 percent of the 1.8 million unit total, more than the next three combined.</desc>
  ...
</svg>
```

- `<title>` must be the first child of `<svg>`.
- IDs must be unique within the document. Prefix them with the figure's own slug.
- The `<desc>` should state the comparison and the focal result, not restate the title.
- Every SVG needs a `viewBox`. Without one it will not scale into a print page.

## Color

- Never communicate a category by color alone. Include a text label.
- Status colors always accompany a written status word or an icon with accessible text.
- Verify every chart in grayscale. If two series become indistinguishable, they needed different shapes, fills, or direct labels — not different hues.
- `print-color-adjust: exact` is a request, not a guarantee. Browsers can still suppress backgrounds, so never let a background color be the only carrier of meaning.

## Contrast

- Body text meets WCAG AA against its surface.
- Large display text meets the applicable WCAG AA threshold.
- Muted text stays genuinely readable. Low contrast to look refined is a failure, not a style.
- Accent-on-accent combinations (`--accent-ink` on `--accent`) meet AA.
- Methodology text (`--method-ink`, `--method-muted` on `--method-bg`) meets AA.

## Interactive elements

- Focus styles are required whenever the document contains links or controls. `core/base.css` provides a `:focus-visible` outline; do not remove it.
- Navigation links need an accessible label.
- A visual limit bar or progress track needs an `aria-label` carrying the exact values, because the shape alone does not convey them.

## Structure

- One `<h1>` per document.
- Heading levels descend without skipping.
- Tables use `<th>` with a real scope, not styled `<td>`.
- Long evidence tables get a `<caption>` naming the population and denominator.

## What to verify

- [ ] Every SVG has a unique title/desc pair and a `viewBox`.
- [ ] No category, series, or status is distinguishable only by hue.
- [ ] The document was checked in grayscale.
- [ ] Body, muted, accent, and methodology text all meet AA.
- [ ] Focus is visible on every link and control.
- [ ] Heading levels form a valid outline.
