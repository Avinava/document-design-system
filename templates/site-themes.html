Wear the client's brand

Point brand-theme-design at a brand guide, a website, a screenshot or a few hex values. Brand colours are chosen for logos and routinely fail as body text, so nothing is eyeballed.

  1. Extract with provenance

    Computed styles from real elements, each value traced to where it came from.

  2. Map to roles

    Mostly subtraction: one accent, the rest mapped to ink, paper, rule and status roles.

  3. Audit every pair

    Contrast at exact thresholds, accent hue clear of the status colours, a print ramp for dark themes.

See how horizon was built. To start your own, copy core/themes/brand-template.css and fill every TODO. The comparison as standalone documents: light · dark.

The token contract is core/tokens.md; every theme declares every token, and audit_theme.py fails a theme that misses one.
