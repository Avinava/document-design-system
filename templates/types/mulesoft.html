Two-minute orientation

The application accepts partner events over HTTP, validates them against the RAML contract, transforms them into the Northwind event envelope, and posts them to /events. It does not own warehouse schema, producer identity policy, or ingestion queue topology.

Boundary: partner-specific shape ends at the transformation. Retry and event identity after that point follow the Northwind Events API.

Evidence and freshness rules

trust
  1. RAML and Mule configuration are the source for listeners, routes, properties, and transformations.
  2. MUnit results support named test claims; absent tests are not inferred passes.
  3. Deployment files own environment names and required configuration.
  4. The Northwind OpenAPI file owns downstream event behavior.
  5. When evidence conflicts, record the mismatch in the owning document instead of smoothing it over here.

Generate only documents supported by real evidence. A short suite with four complete pages is better than a catalog of empty headings.
