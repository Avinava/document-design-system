Coverage and intake

Platform owns business-hours support and the existing on-call path for P1/P2 incidents. Operator questions use the service channel; alerts page on-call.

Priority matrix

PriorityDefinitionFirst owner
P1Acceptance stopped for multiple producersPlatform on-call

Escalation and boundaries

After 15 minutes without falling lag, or on any crash loop, Platform escalates to Reliability and stops replica changes. Checkout, Catalog, and Inventory own payload quality. Warehouse schema stays outside the service.

Runbooks own execution; this page owns who responds and when.
