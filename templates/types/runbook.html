Operational runbook · RB-INGEST-01

Recover when ingestion queue lag keeps climbing

Use this procedure after ingest_lag_seconds exceeds 120 for five minutes or producers report blocked or failed event acceptance.

Owner: Platform Reviewed: 2026-08-18 Maximum local change: +1 replica Escalate at: 15 minutes

Before changing anything

  • Confirm access to the ingest namespace and the Ingestion / lag dashboard.
  • Start an incident note with the page time, current lag, queue depth, replica count, and request p99.
  • Do not restart the gateway or delete a queue.
01

Recovery procedure

target: 15 min

  1. Name the failing boundary

    Open the lag dashboard and identify the queue whose lag is rising. On the current system this is ingest; after RFC 014 it is ingest-a or ingest-b.

    Success: the incident note names one queue, current lag slope, and affected producers.

  2. Check consumer health before scaling

    Inspect ready replicas and restart count. If any consumer is crash-looping, do not scale around it; go directly to escalation.

    bashkubectl -n ingest get deploy/ingest-consumer
kubectl -n ingest get pods -l app=ingest-consumer

    Success: all current replicas are ready and restart count is stable.

  3. Add one consumer replica

    Increase the deployment by exactly one replica. Do not exceed six total replicas without Reliability approval.

    bashkubectl -n ingest scale deploy/ingest-consumer \
  --replicas=$((CURRENT_REPLICAS + 1))

    Success: the new replica becomes ready and lag slope flattens within three minutes.

  4. Observe, then choose

    Watch lag, queue depth, gateway p99, and consumer errors for three minutes.

    If lag falls: continue to verification. If lag is flat: observe for two more minutes. If lag rises or a consumer crashes: escalate.

02

Verify, rollback, or escalate

decision

Verify

Close only on sustained recovery

Lag falls for ten minutes and POST /events p99 remains below 200 ms.

Rollback

Remove the added replica

Return to the previous count if the new replica causes errors. Never delete queues.

Escalate

Stop local changes

After 15 minutes, any crash loop, or rising 5xx: page Platform primary and Reliability.

Record the final replica count, recovery time, peak lag, and whether the documented control changed the slope. Link the incident note before handing back.
