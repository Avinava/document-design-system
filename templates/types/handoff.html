Scope and date

Ownership of the ingestion path: gateway, the current single queue, consumers, and RFC 014 (proposed split). Warehouse ownership stays with Analytics.

What it is

Events enter at POST /events, buffer on ingest, land in the warehouse. Ingestion is 41% of platform footprint. See architecture and RFC 014.

Current status

  • RFC 014 is Proposed, decision by 2026-09-01. Not shipped.
  • Open: consumer rebalance question; shed-versus-block (Reliability).
  • No unreleased branch except the RFC draft. Known bug: lag alert fires at 120s — too late (see postmortem 2026-07-30).

Decisions that bind
