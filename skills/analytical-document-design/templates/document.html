Methodology

Population. 4,180 included records totalling 1,842,110 units. 88 records are excluded: 61 archived before the retention cutoff and 27 with no readable size. Excluded records appear in the supporting CSV.

Dates. "Created" is the record creation timestamp in the source system. "Modified" is the latest recorded change, which may be an automated deployment rather than substantive development.

Classification. Function is inferred from name prefix and source metadata. 3,914 records classified at high confidence, 198 medium, 68 low. Per-record confidence is preserved in the CSV.

Limitations. Cohort charts show current size grouped by creation year. They are not a measure of growth — no historical snapshots exist, so a record that grew after creation is counted entirely in its creation year.
