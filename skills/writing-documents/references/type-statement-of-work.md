**Reader's question:** what services, deliverables, responsibilities, and acceptance are agreed?

## Sections

Context and objective; services and deliverables; schedule and milestones;
client and delivery responsibilities; assumptions and dependencies; exclusions;
acceptance process; commercial basis supplied by the user; change control;
named approvals.

This is delivery-commercial writing, not legal drafting. Never invent parties,
rates, payment terms, liability, warranties, jurisdiction, or boilerplate.
Leave missing terms unresolved and require appropriate review.

## Output

Markdown at `docs/statement-of-work.md`; pattern `contract`, theme
`executive-navy`.
