**Reader's question:** what are we committing to, why, and who can decide?

Use after an initiative is approved but before delivery starts. It converts an
ask into an operating agreement without repeating the statement of work.

## Sections

Purpose and outcomes; measurable success; scope and non-scope; stakeholders
and governance; team and decision rights; milestones; constraints; initial
risks; approval and review cadence.

Do not turn it into a backlog or contract. Name the sponsor, delivery owner,
decision forum, and what happens when scope, time, or cost changes.
