# Workshop summary

```yaml
slug: workshop-summary
title: Workshop summary
aliases: [workshop-readout, meeting-summary, decision-workshop]
example: examples/workshop-summary.html
command: /document-design-system:workshop-summary
pattern: brief
default-theme: field-notes
default-format: markdown
path: docs/workshops/
```

**Reader's question:** what did the workshop establish, decide, and leave open?

Use for a formal, durable workshop readout. Record purpose, date, roles present,
evidence reviewed, conclusions, decisions, assumptions, unresolved questions,
and actions with owners and dates.

Do not transcribe the conversation or invent consensus. Attribute disputed or
provided claims and link durable decisions to ADRs, change requests, or design
documents. Casual meeting-note edits do not need this type.

## Output

Markdown in `docs/workshops/`; pattern `brief`, theme `field-notes`.
