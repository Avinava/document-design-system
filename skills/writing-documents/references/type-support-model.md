**Reader's question:** who supports this service, under what coverage and escalation rules?

Define service boundary, ownership, coverage windows, intake channels, priority
definitions, response expectations, triage responsibilities, escalation path,
vendor boundaries, maintenance, reporting, review cadence, and known gaps.

Do not invent SLAs or contact details. Link procedures to runbooks and transfer
activities to handoff; this document owns the operating agreement.
