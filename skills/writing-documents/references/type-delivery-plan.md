**Reader's question:** how will the agreed outcome be delivered, governed, and forecast?

## Sections

Outcome and baseline; workstreams; milestones and gates; dependencies and
critical path; owners and decision rights; resourcing; quality activities;
risks and assumptions; reporting cadence; change control.

A plan is not a promise that uncertainty disappeared. Make dates, confidence,
dependencies, and forecast movement visible. Execution instructions belong in
a runbook or migration plan.
